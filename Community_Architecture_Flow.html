How a community gets created, structured, and grows on the platform

End-to-end design covering who can start a community, how it's set up, how members join, and what triggers an upgrade prompt. This is a proposal built on top of the Phase 2 CRM/Community scope in the PRD — flag anything that should change.

01

Who can create a community

Creation rights are gated by subscription tier, not by role. Any of the four platform roles — Developer, Investor, Broker, Lender — can create a community once they're on a paid plan. Freemium accounts can join and participate, but can't start one.

Developer
Typically creates market or asset-class communities (e.g. "Bangalore Developers") to network with lenders and investors.
Freemium: join onlySilver+: can create
Investor
Creates investor-only groups, or joins developer communities to source deal flow.
Freemium: join onlySilver+: can create
Broker
Creates regional communities to connect buyers, sellers and both sides of a deal.
Freemium: join onlySilver+: can create
Lender
Creates or joins asset-class communities to stay close to financing demand.
Freemium: join onlySilver+: can create
02

End-to-end flow

From "I want to start a community" to an active, moderated space. The gated step is the only place subscription tier changes the path.

1
Eligibility gate

User taps "Create community"

System checks subscription tier. Freemium sees an upgrade prompt instead of the creation form. Silver/Gold proceed — Silver only if they haven't already used their one community slot (see Section 05).

2

Choose community type

Market-based (e.g. "Mohali & Tricity Builders") or Asset-class-based (e.g. "Apartment Operators"). This drives which filter tab it appears under in the directory.

3

Name, description & visibility

Set the community name, a short description, and choose Open (anyone can join instantly) or Request to join (admin approves each member).

4

Configure channels

Starts with four defaults — #general #approvals #finance #introductions — the creator can rename, remove, or add channels up to their tier's limit.

5

Invite founding members

Creator invites members directly from their contacts/connections, or leaves it empty and relies on directory discovery.

6

Publish — community goes live

Appears in the platform-wide Communities directory, filterable by market or asset class. Creator is automatically the Admin.

7

Ongoing: members post, admin moderates

Members post updates and discuss inside channels; the Admin approves join requests (if Request-to-join), moderates content, and manages channels.

8

Growth hits a cap → upgrade prompt

Member count or channel count approaches the plan's limit — system shows the relevant add-on or tier upgrade (see Section 06).

03

Community anatomy

What a community is actually made of once it exists.

Roles inside a community

AdminThe creator, by default. Approves join requests, moderates posts, manages channels, can promote members to Moderator.
ModeratorGold tier only. Shares moderation/approval workload; cannot change billing or delete the community.
MemberCan post, comment, join channels, and appear in the member directory.

Community types

Market-based

Organized by geography — e.g. "New York Multifamily," "Bangalore Developers." Good for local deal flow and financing.

Asset-class-based

Organized by property type — e.g. "Medical & Healthcare Property," "Mixed-Use Developers." Good for specialist knowledge-sharing.

Default channels
#general#approvals#finance#introductions
04

Member join journey

How someone goes from browsing the directory to being an active member.

Discover

Browse or filter the Communities directory by market or asset class.

Join or request

Open communities: instant join. Request-to-join: sends a request to the Admin.

Approval

Admin accepts or declines (request-to-join only). Instant for open communities.

Active member

Appears in the member list, can post in channels, shows up in "shared communities" on profiles.

06

What happens when a limit is hit

Every cap in this document ends in an in-product prompt, never a silent block.

TriggerWhat the user seesResolution path
Silver user tries to create a 2nd community "You've used your 1 community on the Silver plan." Buy the Extra Community add-on, or upgrade to Gold (includes 3)
Community hits its member cap "This community is at its 150/500 member limit." Buy the Extra Members add-on, or upgrade tier
Gold user tries to create a 4th community "You've used all 3 communities on the Gold plan." Buy an additional Extra Community add-on
Silver Admin wants to delegate moderation "Moderator seats aren't available on Silver." Upgrade to Gold
