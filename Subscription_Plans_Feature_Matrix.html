Draft for review · Subscription Plans

One subscription, five feature pillars: Community, Data Room, Underwriting, Analytics, AI

Freemium, Silver ($25/mo) and Gold ($100/mo) aren't community-only plans — every tier is a ceiling across all five product areas at once. This is a proposal that turns the PRD's phase-by-phase feature list into three purchasable plans plus add-ons. Numbers are a starting point for discussion, not final.

03

Same tiers, every role

Feature ceilings don't change by role — a Developer and an Investor on Gold have the same limits. What changes is which pillar they'll actually push against first.

Developer

Leans on Community + Underwriting hardest — likely to hit the deal-count cap before the data-room storage cap.

Investor

Leans on Analytics + Data Room — benchmark views and external-viewer invites matter more than community creation.

Broker

Leans on Community + CRM — contact cap and member cap are the likely upgrade triggers.

Lender

Leans on Underwriting + Analytics — sensitivity analysis and private data views are Gold-gated features they'll want early.

04

Your specific questions, answered

Can a Developer create only one community?

Yes, on Silver. That's a deliberate single-community ceiling — it's the plan's main upsell lever.

What do they do to create a second one?

Two paths: buy the Extra Community add-on (+$15/mo) and stay on Silver, or upgrade to Gold, which includes 3 communities outright.

How many members are allowed in a Gold ($100) community?

Up to 500 members per community. Beyond that, the Extra Members add-on raises the cap in blocks of 100.

If a Developer signs up, what do they actually get?

Whatever their tier includes across all five pillars — not a Developer-specific bundle. See Section 02 for the exact ceiling at their tier.
